Our Journey

Founded a decade ago, M & A Photography began with a singular vision: to treat wedding photography not as a checklist, but as an editorial art form. We wanted to move away from rigid, traditional posing and instead focus on the authentic, in-between moments that make up the real story of a wedding day.

Today, our studio spans the globe, capturing luxury weddings in over 20 countries. Yet, our core philosophy remains unchanged. We are observers first, directors second.
